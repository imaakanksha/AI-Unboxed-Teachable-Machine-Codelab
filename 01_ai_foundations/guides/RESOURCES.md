# Recommended Resources: Workshop 1 (Foundations of AI) 📚

Here is a curated list of articles, papers, and interactive tools for students wishing to expand their knowledge of AI foundations.

---

## 🧮 How AI Learns & Neural Networks
- **[3Blue1Brown - Neural Networks Series](https://www.3blue1brown.com/topics/neural-networks):** The absolute best visual introduction to how neural networks work, backpropagation, and gradient descent.
- **[TensorFlow Playground](https://playground.tensorflow.org/):** An interactive web-based simulator where you can watch a neural network train in real-time in your browser, experiment with learning rates, regularization, and overfitting.
- **[Backpropagation Calculus (DeepLearning.AI)](https://www.deeplearning.ai/):** Andrew Ng's detailed lectures on mathematics of neural network weight optimization.

---

## 🧠 LLM Mechanics & Stochastic Parrots
- **[On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots (Bender et al., 2021)](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3442188.3445922):** The seminal paper addressing the environmental, ethical, and semantic implications of ultra-large language models.
- **[Attention Is All You Need (Vaswani et al., 2017)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762):** The original research paper introducing the Transformer architecture.

---

## 🔬 Overfitting, Underfitting & regularisation
- **[Bias-Variance Tradeoff (Coursera/Stanford)](https://www.coursera.org/):** Core lectures on generalization error.
- **[Fast.ai - Practical Deep Learning for Coders](https://course.fast.ai/):** Excellent hands-on lessons showing how to tune neural networks to prevent overfitting.

---

## ⚖️ AI Bias & Fairness
- **[IBM AI Fairness 360 (AIF360)](https://aif360.res.ibm.com/):** Open-source toolkit to help examine, report, and mitigate bias in machine learning models.
- **[ProPublica COMPAS Analysis (2016)](https://www.propublica.org/article/machine-bias-risk-assessments-in-criminal-sentencing):** Investigative report on machine bias in criminal sentencing risk models.
- **[Gender Shades (Buolamwini & Gebru, 2018)](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v81/buolamwini18a.html):** Landmark study analyzing intersectional accuracy disparities in commercial gender classification APIs.
