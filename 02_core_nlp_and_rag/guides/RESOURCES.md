# Recommended Resources: Workshop 2 (Core NLP & RAG) 📚

Extend your understanding of Natural Language Processing and RAG architectures with these guides and resources.

---

## 🔤 Tokenizers & Embeddings
- **[Hugging Face Tokenizers Library Guide](https://huggingface.co/docs/tokenizers/index):** Deep dive into BPE, WordPiece, and SentencePiece tokenization implementations.
- **[OpenAI Embeddings Guide](https://platform.openai.com/docs/guides/embeddings):** Best practices for generating and using vector representations for semantic search.
- **[Cohere - What are Embeddings?](https://cohere.com/blog/introduce-embeddings/):** A highly visual, beginner-friendly introduction to vector spaces.

---

## ⚡ Self-Attention & Transformers
- **[The Illustrated Transformer (Jay Alammar)](https://jalammar.github.io/illustrated-transformer/):** The most famous, visually detailed breakdown of Transformers, self-attention, and positional encoding.
- **[Lilian Weng - The Transformer Family](https://lilianweng.github.io/posts/2020-04-07-the-transformer-family/):** A technical summary of various transformer architectures and optimizations.

---

## 💾 Chunking & Vector Databases
- **[Pinecone - Chunking Strategies for LLMs](https://www.pinecone.io/learn/chunking-strategies/):** Excellent explanation of fixed-size, recursive, and semantic chunking.
- **[ChromaDB Documentation](https://docs.trychroma.com/):** Getting started guide for the lightweight, open-source local vector database.
- **[HNSW Indexing Deep Dive](https://www.pinecone.io/learn/series/faiss/hnsw/):** Learn the graph theory and mechanics behind Hierarchical Navigable Small World indexes.

---

## 🔍 Advanced Search, Reranking, & RAG
- **[LangChain - Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)](https://python.langchain.com/docs/use_cases/question_answering/):** Step-by-step guides for building production-grade RAG systems.
- **[Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) paper](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1571941.1572114):** Learn how lexical and vector rankings are merged mathematically.
- **[Cohere Rerank API](https://cohere.com/rerank):** Integrating a reranking model to improve Retrieval Precision.
